Support

A question, a game that looks wrong, a channel missing, a competition you would like to see? Write, in English, French, Portuguese or Spanish.

support@julienmalige.com

It helps to say which version you have (Settings › About › Version), what you saw and when; a photo of the screen says even more. Settings opens from the gear at the top of the menu.

Scores don't move

During a game, scores refresh about every 30 seconds; tennis less often. When fresh scores are late, the app keeps the last ones it received. If nothing changes for several minutes, check that the Apple TV is online.

Choosing your competitions

In Settings › Competitions, select a competition to show or hide it, and select its handle to move it up or down with the remote.

Channels for your country

In Settings › Where to watch, tick France, the United States or Brazil: one, several or none. Each game then shows the channels of the countries you ticked. Settings › Apps to show hides the apps you don't use.

How to Watch opens the App Store

When the app that shows the game isn't installed, or can't be opened from another app, How to Watch opens its page on the App Store instead.

Language

The app follows your Apple TV's language. To choose another one, go to Settings › Language: English, French, Portuguese or Spanish.

What you need

An Apple TV with tvOS 18 or later, connected to the internet. The app is free, with no ads, no purchases and no account.
