Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

TV Scores has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it collects no personal data. Here is everything that leaves your Apple TV, and where it goes.

What the app asks for

To show scores, the app asks the TV Scores server (tvscores.julienmalige.com) for the games, the tables and the pictures. Each request carries your Apple TV's time zone, so that games fall on the right day, and nothing about you: no name, no email address, no identifier.

What the server keeps

The sports data it serves, and nothing about who asks: no IP address, no device identifier, no history of what you looked at.

Cloudflare

Requests reach the server through Cloudflare, which carries and protects the traffic. Like any network that carries web traffic, Cloudflare sees your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Pictures

Crests, photos and app icons come from the TV Scores server. Now and then, when the server does not have a picture yet, your Apple TV loads it from the site that publishes it, such as TheSportsDB, which sees your IP address as any website would.

What stays on your Apple TV

Your choices (the competitions and their order, the countries, the apps and the language) are saved on the Apple TV and never sent anywhere.

Other apps

How to Watch opens another app, a broadcaster's or a streaming service's, or its page on the App Store. What happens there is up to that app and its own privacy policy.

Apple

If you chose, in your Apple TV's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send anonymous crash reports and usage figures for TV Scores. That is Apple's doing, under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off in those settings.

Test versions

Test versions handed out through Apple's TestFlight also send the server an anonymous record of how the remote moves through the app (what took the focus, which menu opened) to find and fix navigation faults. It is tied to a random number made when the app is installed, not to you. The App Store version sends none of it.

Children

TV Scores collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page will say so first, with a new date.
